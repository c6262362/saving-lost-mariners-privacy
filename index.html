SAVING LOST MARINERS

Privacy Policy

A Series of Maritime Navigation and Ocean Visualization Applications

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Saving Lost Mariners application series, including:

  • Saving Lost Mariners — a 3D celestial navigation and almanac workstation for exploring celestial bodies, navigation observations, and astronomical positioning.
  • SLM – Tides — a 3D Earth and tidal visualization application featuring the Sun, Moon, global ports, tide stations, and available tidal information.

Both applications are designed to be usable without an account. They do not include advertisements, in-app purchases, or third-party analytics.

Location

Where location services are supported, an application may access your device location only when you explicitly request a location-based function and grant permission.

Location is used on your device for the relevant application function, such as setting an observation position or locating nearby ports. Your device location is not transmitted to the developer.

You may instead enter a position manually where supported. Location permissions can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

Observations, application data and preferences

Application data, including navigation observations, selected positions, tide-related information, and interface preferences, is handled locally on your device where applicable.

The applications do not provide developer-operated user accounts or cloud synchronization. Personal observation entries, manually selected positions, and interface preferences are not transmitted to the developer.

Some applications may include official or independently sourced geographical, astronomical, and tidal datasets for offline reference and visualization. These datasets are application resources and are distinct from personal user data.

Retention and control

Locally stored settings and user-created entries remain on your device until changed, cleared, or the relevant application is removed.

Where available, you may use in-app controls to clear observations or other locally stored information. Removing an application deletes data held within its app container.

Apple may separately process download, purchase, and diagnostic information in accordance with Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

Third-party services and data sources

The applications may use publicly available astronomical, geographical, oceanographic, and tidal data from their respective sources.

The inclusion of third-party reference datasets does not mean that personal user information is shared with those data providers. Any applicable source attribution and licensing information is provided within the relevant application or its documentation.
