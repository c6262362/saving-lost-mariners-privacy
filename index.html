Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Saving Lost Mariners is a celestial navigation education and visualization app. The app can be used without an account and does not include advertisements, in-app purchases, or third-party analytics.

Location

With your permission, the app accesses your device location when you request positioning. Location is used on your device to set an observation position. Your location is not transmitted to the developer. You may instead enter a position manually, and you may change location permissions in iOS Settings.

Observations and preferences

Observation entries and navigation values are handled on your device. Interface preferences may be saved in local app storage. The app does not offer developer-operated cloud syncing or user accounts and does not transmit these details to the developer.

Retention and control

Local settings remain until changed, cleared, or the app is removed. You can use available controls to clear observations, or remove the app to delete data held in its app container. Apple may process download and diagnostic information separately under Apple's privacy terms.
